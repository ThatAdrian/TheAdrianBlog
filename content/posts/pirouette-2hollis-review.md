## The Sound

Fabulous album, i didnt expect any less from 2hollis. The first song on my current playlist from February 2025 was jeans and i was listening to it since before that playlist, id say hes an artist that really awakened me to the genre and the electronic side of music. This album has a great variety of tracks from upbeat tracks like Pressure or My One and Only to slower tracks like today they all go so well, i also admire how he released some old unreleased tracks that have been circulating around the internet for a while such as Warhorse and Daisy. Both are slightly revamped and improved. The only downside i have is that the lady in red rap part brings it down a couple points, i think its corny. but everything else from the sound of it and how he continues his regular sound without making it seem overdone or repetitive to the amazing artistry throughout the album rollout and artwork associated.
