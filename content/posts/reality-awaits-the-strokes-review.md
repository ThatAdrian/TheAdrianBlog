## The Sound

I was rather excited for this album. It's not horrible as it is now but I stand quite firmly that his autotune ruins most of the songs it's on. That is strongly seen in Going Shopping. This album would be a flop if not for the couple of standouts, such as the masterpiece that is Dine n Dash. That song is an instant classic and a couple decent ones such as the fruits of conquest and going to babble on. they all use a lot less autotune and the vocals are manageable. if not for that one point i think this album would've been a lot better.

Either way, im going to see this live soon and im still quite excited. I've given this album a handful of listens by now and it's okay; I don't skip anything yet, but the only one that is going on my playlist is Dine n Dash.

## Standout Tracks

Dine N Dash, The Fruits of Conquest and Going To Babble On

## Verdict

I wanted something more unfortunately, it all rounds up to being nothing too special, go listen to Dine n Dash though. As an album it's not horrible but my expectations were higher.
