## The Sound

Beautiful album, ive loved it since first listen, it has made me cry throughout the whole first half of the album.

The composition and performance of all of the tracks is insane and so well made, ive kept my eye on the singles when they released and enjoyed those since then also, i saw Quadeca at AllPointsEast this year which was very nice but he definitley deserves a bigger later set.

Honestly it was an unexpected album in the sense that its not what i expected to hear, the strings, the winds, the natural feel and the raw and rough basic sounds like shown in the hero never dies are all amazing accents to an amazing album.

## Standout Tracks

Make you my what, it goes like this, the hero never dies, dark magic and hell of a time

## Verdict

I say it's a must listen, I'm excited to see what comes next and I will mourn the putting down of the Quadeca mask as he has mentioned in interviews as i believe it's going up and up for Ben
