## The Sound

It's an alright release from Mr Lacy, i think he is being overshadowed by Malcolm Todd, who i believe released a better album this year compared to this, Oh Yeah? is a decent album and i remember a long long time ago when Nice Shoes came out originally i was playing that song nonstop. I think that song is very good although the album version with the second half is in my opinion, a slight downgrade, i prefer the original.

Either way, the rollout was strange; I'm not sure why he released the first single so long ago before the real rollout started happening but nevertheless the other two singles was also very good and I was playing them a lot in recent days. 

Apart from the 3 singles, I don't think the rest of the album is as interesting but it's not a bad release at all. Simply wouldn't say I'm sufficed.

## Standout Tracks

Nice Shoes, the feeling and is it cool?

## Verdict

Yeah it will do. Replay value isn't crazy for the whole album but the 3 singles are great.
