## The Sound

Honestly, some of their songs came up on shuffle and it piqued my interest, then another and then another and i decided to listen to the album itself.

I think it's a really solid album, it kept me entertained and it's quite varied. I didn't feel like any of the songs were the same and a handful even made it onto the playlist. The ladies vocals are great and im surprised I haven't heard of them before.

Some of the tracks are a bit lacking, hence the low score for a couple or it's kinda going into dnb which im not that interested in etc etc. Other than that, not much more to say. It's quite solid.
